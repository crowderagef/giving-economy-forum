Giving Economy Forum

About Us

The Giving Economy Forum celebrates the convergence of industries from social impact, startups, philanthropy, nonprofit, corporate and media.

Who we are

The World's First Giving Economy Changemakers Summit

In the grand tapestry of human achievement, there's a thread that weaves the stories of those who give, who share, and who strive to uplift every corner of our world. This thread is brightly illuminated at the Giving Economy Changemakers (GEC) Awards and Summits, an annual celebration of the extraordinary.

We are more than an event. We are a catalyst for the dreamers, creators and visionaries who dare to ask, "How can we make the world a better place?" At the heart of the GEC, we spotlight the convergence of wisdom from world leaders and changemakers from technology, media, charity, corporate and entertainment, converging for a single purpose: to usher in a new era where abundance begins with the act of giving.

At our core lies a commitment to a world where kindness, generosity and benevolence echo far and wide. Our vision reaches beyond any one event: it places humanity and positive impact at the centre, so that giving and thriving become not just ideals but a way of life.

We aspire to embody Sustainable Development Goal 17, a universal call for partnership. The GEC is a powerhouse for building global partnerships, enhancing social capital, and advancing technology to accelerate positive change, nurturing causes with technology, training and social capital through 2030 and beyond.

Our impact

A Formidable Force for Good

Since its inception, the GEC has recognised and learned from changemakers across the United States, India, Africa and Southeast Asia, now more than 250 awardees. From human trafficking activists to innovative social entrepreneurs, they tackle issues from LGBTQ+ rights to education and sanitation with unwavering dedication.

The impact goes beyond the awards ceremony. By bringing their stories to a global audience, the GEC fosters a sense of community and encourages more changemakers to rise up, lighting the way to a more equitable, compassionate world.

About Crowdera

Established in 2014 in Silicon Valley, Crowdera is a global online fundraising platform on a mission to amplify the collective impact of philanthropy and social entrepreneurship.

Crowdera's core mission is to enable and nurture social impact initiatives by providing a fee-free fundraising platform. It is the bridge connecting nonprofits and social innovators with the resources they need, helping fundraisers foster authentic connections, expand their reach and grow their donor base.

Rooted in a commitment to the UN Sustainable Development Goals, its reach spans the globe, with payment gateway partnerships that let the platform function in 200 countries.

Since 2018

The Giving Economy Movement

The very first Giving Economy Changemakers Summit was kicked off on #GivingTuesday, 27 November 2018, at 91 SpringBoard BKC, Mumbai. It was also the formal kickoff of "The Giving Economy" movement.

Since then, Crowdera Foundation has hosted the Giving Economy Summit & Awards every year, focusing on innovation, creativity and entrepreneurship, and bringing together leading philanthropists, entrepreneurs, social innovators, activists, artists and NGOs to lead transformative change toward SDG implementation.

The main aim of the movement is to raise awareness of the UN Sustainable Development Goals to be achieved by 2030, for the welfare and enrichment of people, fellow earthlings and the planet.
